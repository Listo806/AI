Is annual billing really $67 per month?

$67 is the monthly equivalent. You pay $804 upfront for one year. Monthly billing is $97 each month, or $1,164 over 12 months. Annual billing saves $360, approximately 31%.

Do the plans include different features?

No. This is one plan with two billing choices. Choosing monthly does not remove charting features; choosing annual reduces the effective monthly price.

Not yet. The current chart has rule-based readings. Connected AI and calendar-aware analysis require backend integration and testing. AI availability and usage limits will be disclosed before activation; unlimited AI is not promised.

Can I subscribe on this page today?

Not yet. This page shows the approved prices and billing choices. Your selection opens an order summary, but it does not create a subscription or collect payment. Secure checkout, account access and billing management will be connected before sales open.

What are the cancellation and refund terms?

Final renewal, cancellation and refund terms will be clearly available before checkout opens. Annual billing is one upfront payment for a year, not a monthly installment arrangement.
